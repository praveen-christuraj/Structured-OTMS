# Login

## Purpose

Use this screen to sign in to OTMS.

## Who can access

- Everyone (this is the entry point before you are signed in).

## How to use

1. Enter your **username** and **password**
2. If your account has **2FA enabled**, complete the 2FA prompt
3. After successful login, you are routed to `Home`

## Common first-login requirements

Depending on the account policy set by administrators, you may be required to:
- change your password on first login
- set up 2FA immediately

## Troubleshooting

- **Invalid credentials:** confirm username/password and check caps lock.
- **2FA issues:** use backup codes if available, or request support via `My Tasks` / admins.
